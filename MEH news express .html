New Technology: New Phones?
iPhone 17 vs Samsung S26

As we move deeper into 2026, the two giants of the smartphone world are once again going head-to-head. Whether you are a die-hard iOS fan or an Android enthusiast, this year's flagship battle is shaping up to be more about intelligence than just hardware.

The Display & Design

iPhone 17: Rumors suggest Apple is finally introducing the "Slim" or "Air" model, focusing on a razor-thin chassis and a more integrated under-display FaceID to maximize screen real estate.

Samsung S26: Samsung continues to dominate screen technology. Expect an even brighter AMOLED panel with improved "Vision Booster" tech, making it the clear winner for outdoor visibility.

The Power of AI

The real battleground isn't megapixels — it's Silicon.

iPhone 17's A19 chip is expected to prioritize local, private AI processing, making Siri more of a personal collaborator than a voice assistant.

Samsung S26 is likely to double down on its Galaxy AI ecosystem, offering real-time video translation and even more advanced generative photo editing tools.

Camera Innovations

Samsung typically wins on zoom, while Apple wins on video. This year:

• Samsung is rumored to be refining its 200MP sensor for better low-light performance.

• Apple is reportedly upgrading the front-facing camera for the first time in years, moving to a 24MP sensor for ultra-crisp selfies and professional-grade video calls.

New Program Launches — The Latest Updates

As the long-haul aircraft's A340 and the classical B747 era silently fades away, the two manufacturers (Airbus and Boeing) launched two new projects — the A350-1000 and the B777X. Both aircrafts are suitable for long haul flights.

Engines & Efficiency

The A350 is powered by Rolls-Royce Trent XWB engines, while the B777X uses only GE9X engines. This gives the A350 a notable advantage in fuel efficiency and reliability on long-haul routes.

The Verdict

For long haul flights, modern design is more important than raw capacity. Because the A350-1000 delivers both fuel efficiency and a future-forward feel, it stands out as the more suitable aircraft for the next generation of long-haul aviation.

Fun Learning in Singapore

32 students were chosen to go to Singapore for a learning trip from 28th March to 1st April. Here's Silas's full account of every unforgettable day.

🦁🌃🎡🌊🏫 Singapore Learning Trip · 4 Days · 32 Students

Day 1 — Arrival & Merlion Park

We arrived at Singapore after our 4-hour long haul flight. After we filled our tummies and took our luggage and put them in the car, we went straight off to the Merlion Park to take pictures. We ate our dinner at the local food center. It was very easy for us to sleep as we woke up at 4 o'clock to catch the plane.

Day 2 — School Visit & Chinatown

We refreshened ourselves and visited Greenwood Primary School. The students and teachers are all very kind and nice and treated us with wide and joyful smiles. My buddy, Prinz Oh, led me through the whole school like a tour guide. He even introduced very yummy snacks for me!

After that, we attended a persuasive speech training class. Later, we bought lots of souvenirs in Chinatown.

Day 3 — Camp Challenge

We went to a camp challenge and we were all exhausted. We played many games that strengthened our teamwork and collaborating skills. The most memorable game we played was the balance beam — all the boys had to stand on the wide beam and try to balance it. The one who balances longer wins.

We improved by communicating with each other, while we adjusted our center of gravity and the place we were standing on. After our lunch — which was a simple boxed lunch — we made a raft with plastic floaters, tubes and strings. We then rafted it in the open sea to test if it actually works. It was a draining day and I enjoyed it very much.

Day 4 — Departure & Marina Barrage

We headed straight for Marina Barrage after our breakfast because we didn't want to waste any time as it was already the last day of the Singapore trip. We learned a lot about the electricity and water cycle there. I even learned that although Hong Kong is 1.5 times bigger than Singapore, there are 17 reservoirs there!

Afterwards, we drove about 5-10 minutes to get to the pork ribs soup restaurant. The soup was spectacular and amazing — I drank 4 bowls! Last but not least, we took beautiful pictures in the HSBC rain vortex and bought lots of souvenirs at the airport.

"Although Singapore was very humid and hot, I still enjoyed this trip with my classmates and teachers. I hope I will have more similar experiences in the future!"

— Silas L.

Delta Force Challenges PUBG Mobile's Dominance

The mobile gaming landscape is witnessing a seismic shift. Delta Force Mobile has crossed $300M revenue in its first five months — is PUBG Mobile's reign finally under threat?

🔺
DELTA FORCE
TiMi Studio · Garena
VS
🔵
PUBG MOBILE
Krafton · Level Infinite

A New Breed of Combat

While PUBG Mobile popularized the Battle Royale genre, Delta Force (formerly Delta Force: Hawk Ops) is carving a different path by focusing on high-fidelity tactical realism and diverse gameplay modes:

  • ▸Hazard Operations: A next-gen "Extraction Shooter" mode where players must loot and escape, adding a high-stakes economy.
  • ▸Warfare Mode: Massive 64-player battles (32v32) across land, sea, and air — tanks, helicopters, armored vehicles. Strikingly similar to Battlefield.
  • ▸Black Hawk Down: A nostalgic campaign mode offering narrative-driven PvE missions.

"We are seeing a major shift in player tastes. Gamers are moving toward deeper tactical coordination rather than just the 'last man standing' loop." — Leo Yao, head of TiMi I3 Studio

PUBG Mobile's Response

Despite the surge of Delta Force, PUBG Mobile remains a cultural titan. Industry analysts suggest that while some "hardcore" tactical players are migrating, PUBG Mobile's accessibility and Battle Royale purity keep it at the top in India and Southeast Asia.

  • ▸Blue Lock Crossover (May 12): A 25-level "Prize Path" with themed character sets and weapon finishes from the hit soccer anime.
  • ▸FIFA World Cup Hype: Football-themed event from May 22 with sports mini-games on Erangel.
  • ▸"PAYDAY" Heist Mode (May 12–June 10): A high-intensity bank heist collaboration with Starbreeze Entertainment featuring a soundtrack by 070 Shake.

Delta Force — Burst Fest 2026

  • ▸New "Overload" Mode: Stack buffs through killstreaks across three intensifying combat levels.
  • ▸Tactical Looting: Treasure Hunting mechanic + Dog Tag Exchanges for exclusive Faction Medals.
  • ▸Esports Push: Operations Pro League launched in China with 24 elite teams, building toward a World Cup in November 2026.

The Technical Edge

Delta Force Mobile supports 144FPS gameplay on high-end devices, outperforming PUBG Mobile and BGMI. The 1:1 scale map replications and superior lighting effects offer an immersion level previously reserved for PC titles.

The "War" for Your Storage

• Want extraction-style tension and 144FPS? Delta Force is the current leader.

• Want variety, massive player counts, and fun anime crossovers? PUBG Mobile still holds the crown.

The "Shooter Wars" of 2026 are just heating up. Mobile gamers have never had better options for high-quality warfare on the go.
